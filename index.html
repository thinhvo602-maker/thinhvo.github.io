Projects

Global Sales Performance Analysis

Power BI

Problem
Analyzed global sales efficiency, customer engagement, diagnosed the reasons for revenue decline and provided actionable recommendations to optimize revenue and profit, improve customer experience, and enhance sales performance in countries.
Data
Global Electronics Retailer dataset with five tables covering 2016 to 2021.
Approach
  • Built an interactive multi-page dashboard by using DAX and designing appropriate data modelling for Tables. Visualized Business metrics (Revenue, Profit, ARPU, Repeat rate) and analyzed KPIs by product, customer, country, and time dimensions.
  • Used DAX Measures for advanced analytical approach: Cohort retention and Customer Segmentation.
  • Analyzed through Overview -> Details Framework to identify business problems and provided actions to improve business performance.
Key insights
  • Active Customers (New, Returning) were the key drivers of revenue decline ($5,7M Q4 2019 -> $1M Q1 2021).
  • 80% of Revenue came from 40% of Product Subcategories, leading to dependency risks.
  • 21.08% Customers at risk of churn (contributing $10.38M total revenue), Older cohorts gradually reached the end of their lifecycle, new cohorts declined immediately.

International Freight Transportation Performance Analysis

SQL Python Power BI

Problem
Analyzed international freight transport efficiency, diagnosed the problems of Revenue, Customers and Operations and provided actionable recommendations to optimize revenue, improve customer experience, and enhance operational performance.
Data
A six-table dataset (route, product type, customer order, product, fact_link, fact_warehouse) from 11/2025 to 3/2026, including data errors needed to be cleaned.
Approach
  • Used Python to build a data pipeline that loads raw data into BigQuery. Queried, cleaned tables using SQL and used the SQL Queries to load cleaned data into Power BI.
  • 58 records in the `fact_warehouse` table were found to have invalid time logic and were excluded from the lead time analysis to avoid skewing the results.
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with DAX measures to visualize Business Metrics (Revenue, Lead time, Completion rate, Repeat rate).
  • Analyzed through Overview -> Details Framework to identify business problems and provided recommendations to improve business performance.
Key insights
  • Customers (New, Returning) and Customer Retention were the drivers of revenue decline.
  • A significant number of customers dropped off at the stage of transitioning to their second (49.78%) and third orders (33.33%).
  • The company encountered a bottleneck at the warehousing stage in Vietnam (11.64 days), resulting in long lead times.
