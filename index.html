Projects

Global Sales Performance Analysis

Industry: Sales, Retail - Technologies used: Power BI

Problem
Analyze global sales efficiency, customer engagement and diagnose the reasons for revenue decline, give actions to optimize revenue and profit, improve customer experience, and enhance sales performance in countries.
Data
Global Electronics Retailer dataset with five tables covering 2016 to 2021.
Approach
  • Built an interactive multi-page dashboard by using DAX and designing appropriate data modelling for Tables. Visualized Business metrics (Revenue, Profit, ARPU, Repeat rate) and analyzed Metrics through Dimensions using simple and advanced charts.
  • Used DAX Measures for advanced analytical approach: Cohort retention and Customers Segmentation.
  • Analyze through Overview -> Details Framework to identify business problems and provided actions to improve business performance.
Key insights
  • Active Customers (New, Returning) are the key drivers of revenue decline ($5,7M Q4 2019 -> $1M Q1 2021).
  • 80% of Revenue comes from 40% of Product Subcategories, leading to dependency risks.
  • 21.08% Customers at risk of churn (contributing $10.38M total revenue), Older cohorts gradually reach the end of their lifecycle, new cohorts decline immediately.

International Freight Transportation Performance Analysis

Industry: Logistics, Supply Chain - Technologies used: SQL(Bigquery), Power BI, Python

Problem
Analyze international freight transport efficiency, diagnose the problems of Revenue, Customers and Operations and give actions in order to optimize revenue, improve customer experience, and enhance operational performance.
Data
A six-table dataset (route, product type, customer order, product, fact_link, fact_warehouse) from 11/2025 to 3/2026, including data errors needed to be cleaned.
Approach
  • Used Python to build a data pipeline that loads raw data into BigQuery. Queried, cleaned tables using SQL and used the SQL Queries to load cleaned data into Power BI.
  • 58 records in the `fact_warehouse` table were found to have invalid time logic and were excluded from the lead time analysis to avoid skewing the results.
  • Built an interactive Power BI dashboard with DAX measures to visualize Business Metrics (Revenue, Lead time, Completion rate, Repeat rate) and analyze, diagnose the problems.
  • Analyzed through Overview -> Details Framework to identify business problems and provided actions to improve business performance.
Key insights
  • Customers (New, Returning) and Customer Retention are the drivers of revenue decline.
  • A significant number of customers drop off at the stage of transitioning to their second (49.78%) and third orders (33.33%).
  • The company encountered a bottleneck at the warehousing stage in Vietnam (11.64 days), resulting in long lead times.
